Bujhi

Natural English ⇄ বাংলা, anywhere on the web.

  1. Select text on any page, or click into a text box you're typing in.
  2. Press Alt+B or click the Bujhi icon.
  3. Copy, listen, or replace your typed Bangla with natural English.
  4. Typing in a box? Alt+Shift+B translates and replaces it instantly (Ctrl+Z undoes).
